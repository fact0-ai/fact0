---
title: "Audit evidence limits"
description: "Recorded evidence does not certify compliance."
---

Fact0 supplies execution records, an audit hash chain and signed exports that may help an organization investigate activity. It does not certify SOC 2, HIPAA or another compliance framework, establish runtime safety, or guarantee complete capture.

Use [verification and exports](/guides/verification) to understand exactly what is checked. Assess legal, organizational and technical controls independently.
